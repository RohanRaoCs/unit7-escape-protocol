UNIT-7 Escape Protocol

An AI agent escapes a facility that keeps changing around it. It collects three keycards, solves a lock puzzle at a terminal and reaches the exit, while a drone hunts it, laser gates switch on and off, coolant spreads, ceilings collapse onto its route and power surges rewrite the lock's rules.

AI techniques

TechniqueJobModule
A* search (space and time)Plans routes and times its way through laser gates; Dijkstra, greedy and BFS for comparisonsearch.py
Constraint satisfactionGenerates and solves the vault lock with backtracking, forward checking, MRV and AC-3csp.py
Behaviour treeDecides what the agent does each tickbt.py
Fuzzy logic (Mamdani)Rates how dangerous each cell isfuzzy.py
Finite state machineControls the drone: Patrol, Alert, Chase, Cooldowndrone.py
